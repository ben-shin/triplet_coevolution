# Research platform completion audit

This is the implementation report through Phase 10. It traces the owner's numbered specification against the local research branch. Phase 11 still has an independent scientific gate. An independently staged cohort, a passing software test and a measured experimental result answer different questions. The baseline and phase-by-phase records identify the commits, commands, datasets, numerical differences and failed approaches. [Specification](implementation-specification.md) · [Original source audit](specification-audit.md) · [Implementation ledger](implementation-ledger.md) · [Verification record](validation.md)

## What a researcher can run

Build an offline example with `triplet init --template protein-rna-rna --out example`. Run `triplet score --config example/analysis.json --out example/scored`. Use `triplet report --run example/scored --out example/report` to regenerate the report without fitting. For new evidence, `triplet benchmark --scores example/scored --labels example/labels.tsv --out example/bench` works from the durable pair-score archive. Each result identifies its coordinate universe and statistic. [Full CLI and Python example](python-api.md)

The main changes are in `src/triplet_coevolution/{inference,validation,msa,io,evidence,search,structure,reporting}`, the file-backed analysis service, the local workflow runner and its CLI. Numerical and data-integrity tests live in `tests/`. Independent measurements, timings and reproducible drivers live in `benchmarks/`. Actual software versions, opt-in paths, skip reasons and comparisons to legacy appear in the verification record. [Tests](../tests/) · [Measurements](../benchmarks/) · [Validation](validation.md)

| §4.1. QC, depth, weighting and masks | Source-coordinate masks, synchronized filtering with ordered passes, taxon/clade counts, row/site diagnostics and row-specific weights, neighbors or memberships are persisted. Exact overlap/identity and count/mass thresholds are enforced in fits, nulls, APC and ranking. Six weighting policies are compared on a fixed biological panel. [QC](qc.md) · [Depth comparison](../benchmarks/weighting-policies.md) | Effective depth is descriptive. Metadata can be unavailable. Clade correction and reweighting have different assumptions. No unvalidated approximate neighbor search is substituted. |
| §4.2. Mean-field alternatives | Ridge inverse covariance retains primal/dual paths, covariance units, declared alphabet/gap treatment, joint vs interface conditioning and sensitivity grids. A fixed development panel demonstrates shifts in ranks and support. [Estimator options](conditional-models.md) · [Biological sensitivity](../benchmarks/gatabc-conditioning.md) | Conditioning changes and shrinkage sensitivity do not identify a direct triple. Prespecified grid settings remain visible. Selection on development labels yields no unbiased test estimate. |
| §4.3. PLM | Asymmetric mixed categorical conditionals have separate field/coupling penalties, stable optimization, symmetrization, memory guards, support and convergence diagnostics. Confirmed on exact indirect systems and independently measured against pinned protein-only plmc. [PLM reference and limits](plm.md) | The optional missing-predictor mean imputer induced a false edge in an exact control and is disabled. Complete cases can lose substantial depth. Full mixed-protein fits can exceed RAM or fail convergence. No silent estimator switch occurs. |
| §5.1. Marginal hierarchy | Fixed-support IPF fits the pairwise maximum-entropy model. Connected information is KL in bits. Signed co-information and zero-sum log-contrast RMS have separate units and directions. Priors, pooling, residuals, occupancy, likelihood, degeneracy and structural zeros are explicit. Exact XOR, pair-only, constant and infeasible controls are tested. [Definitions and experiments](maxent.md) · [Native analysis](local-analysis.md) | An iid, local marginal fit has no thermodynamic interpretation. Smoothing toward M2 or outcome-derived priors lacks a validated selection protocol. Nonconvergence or inadequate support yields an unavailable statistic. |
| §5.2. Conditional higher-order model | Sparse pair-only and pair-plus-selected-K fits share genuinely held-out rows, graph, alphabets and penalties. Saved models can score new data. Clade-level uncertainty and bounded exact simulation accompany conditional loss. [Held-out experiments](../benchmarks/nested-models.md) · [Workflow](conditional-models.md) | The gain estimates conditional predictive improvement. Unsupported missingness, large-state ensemble MCMC, validated group sparsity and a physical interpretation of K remain outside the implemented estimand. Independent biological nested comparisons are a Phase 11 requirement. |
| §5.3. Candidate selection | Exhaustive, uniform, explicit, prior, triangle/wedge, entropy and hybrid modes have bounded scans, source coordinates, stable identities and selection hashes. Training-only screens and a score-independent quota preserve a route to pure synergy. Candidate coverage is reported. [Discovery recall experiment](../benchmarks/candidate-discovery.md) | Budgeted search can miss the largest true triple. Same-data selection is exploratory. General selective inference by re-screening on every eligible null draw is unavailable. |
| §6.1. Resampling | Whole-component, within-stratum and pair-preserving exchanges have explicit exchangeability diagnostics. Fixed-support iid M2 and exact small joint pair-model bootstraps refit under their stated laws. Independent-site F81 uses supplied branch lengths, masks and matched tips. Clade splits/bootstrap estimate stability. Conservative directional p-values, BH/BY, applicable familywise maxT, MC uncertainty, finite resolution, invalid-draw counts, resume and deterministic shards are tested. [Calibration](calibration.md) · [Size and power](null-models.md) · [Tree results](tree-model.md) | Conditional fixed-margin table sampling, large joint-state MCMC, pair-coupled phylogenetic evolution and general data-dependent sequential stopping lack validation. Missing or single-stratum exchangeability can make a claim untestable. Interim samples are exploratory. Marginal iid bootstrap and independent-site tree nulls test different hypotheses. |
| §7. Physical structure and experimental labels | Native assembly transformations, models, copies, full-polymer alignment, ambiguous mappings, modified nucleotides, author coordinates and missing observations drive complete distance/status tables. Source kind is explicit. Heavy-atom, protein CA/CB, threshold/negative-control sensitivity, candidate geometry, crossed labels and script exports are available. 2G5H yielded 2,830 agreements, 13 measured differences and 37 unobservable among 2,880 historical labels. [Mapping](structure-mapping.md) · [Independent calculations](../benchmarks/gatabc-structure.md) | Explicit anchors are supported. Automatic validated SIFTS ingestion, a comprehensive support- and pair-score-matched triple-control study, ensemble confidence across arbitrary predicted structures, independent validated biochemical perturbation measurements and complete independent-system performance are open. Native viewer files have coordinate/script fixture checks. Executing third-party viewer GUIs requires a separate local installation. |
| §8.1. Search, selection and alignment | Disk-indexed FASTA, A3M and Stockholm retain alignment, insertion and domain coordinates. Native search supports local reference projection, iterative JACKHMMER/search profiles, RNA CMs, PSI-BLAST, domain table import and selected profile-aware hmmalign/cmalign. Actual HMMER, Infernal and BLAST+ runs, two directions, versioned raw search, cache invalidation and independent copies are exercised. [Workflow inputs](workflows.md) · [Executable evidence](../benchmarks/search-discovery.md) | MAFFT, proteome-wide reciprocal orthology and large uncurated search-quality benchmarks are optional extensions without validation. The legacy best-domain selection is intentionally versioned. A score alone cannot establish partnering. |
| §8.2. Biological pairing and metadata | Curated tables, accession/provider aliases, GFF3 loci, physical intervals/strands, explicit systems/operons and RNA context are reconciled across all candidates. Ambiguous and incomplete candidates survive in the audit. Network lookups use public IDs, bounded/cached fetches and explicit failures. Offline replay is supported. [Evidence schema](evidence.md) · [Genome mapping](genomic-evidence.md) | Taxonomy alone is insufficient. Distinct eukaryotic compartments/isoforms or repeated bacterial/RNA loci often require curated evidence. There is no validated covariance-based paralog assignment, arbitrary cross-provider join or async job submission engine. Direct paginated lookups use their own retry protocol. Provider updates can require refreshed caches. |
| §9.1. I/O and planning | Exact selected rows and coordinate references are identified before numerical allocation. [Ingestion](native-ingestion.md) · [Plan interpretation](analysis-planning.md) | The selected numerical matrices still require memory. Native plan and process RSS are different quantities. | §9.2. Exact performance | Mixed-alphabet optimized kernels have exact-reference tests, per-engine budget checks and same-host timing. [Kernel settings, intervals and exceptions](kernel-performance.md) | Speeds depend on hardware/BLAS and the chosen estimator. Optional Cholesky can change near-tie ordering and is rejected under historical legacy mode. GPU, approximate clusters and compiled optional replacements have no parity claim. |

# Experimental benchmark protocol

The machine-readable plan fixes the files, copy choices, evaluation populations, thresholds and admission rules before the new coordinates are scored. File hashes identify the registration. This is a local prospective record for the new comparisons. Previous project results for the original GatABC structure were already known. [Protocol](../benchmarks/experimental_protocol.json)

## GatABC structural replication

Apply the same four frozen pair rankings from the 888-observation matched alignment to two other experimental structures. Evaluate assembly 1, model 1. In 3AL0 the third subunit is fused to a ligase. Use the deposited GatC database segment exclusively. In 3KFU the deposited chain order specifies two putative trimers, E/F/G and H/I/J. Verify subunit entity identities, copy labels and homolog correspondence. Flag disagreement between declared copy membership and gross chain placement; retain all prespecified copies and abstain if membership cannot be established. The two copies describe one deposition. Neither deposition supplies independent sequence predictions. [Thermotoga structure](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3AL0) · [Thermus structure](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3KFU)

Phase 2 uses global affine-gap BLOSUM62 alignment, tracks equally optimal correspondences and uses only resolved residues. A positive alignment score, at least 25% identity and 60% coverage of uniquely aligned reference positions are required for the independently annotated homologs. Report the native 30%/70% gate as a sensitivity. Calculate minimum positive-occupancy heavy-atom distances for all AB, AC and BC reference pairs. Missing coordinates or ambiguous alignment positions give unknown labels. At 8 Å, report counts, natural prevalence, common-denominator average precision and AUROC, interface macro means and coverage of the top 10 and top 50. Vary the cutoff over 4, 5, 8 and 10 Å. Report all copies and the intersection of observable, jointly scoreable pairs across copies. Do not pool copies as independent experiments.

## Externally inferred tree and independent systems

Use the versioned GTDB bacterial marker tree and exact assembly witnesses from a local join to bulk public records. Record every exclusion, archive source checksums and keep observation identifiers local. Prune the tree to uniquely admitted tips, summing contracted branch lengths. Compare raw mutual information on the prespecified small site panel against an empirical-frequency independent-site tree null and against held-out phyla. These tests assess dependence conditional on a model and the retained missing-data patterns. [GTDB release 232](https://data.gtdb.ecogenomic.org/releases/release232/232.0/) · [UniProt proteome fields](https://rest.uniprot.org/configure/proteomes/result-fields)

The independent-system track registers E. coli RNA polymerase, the mixed 5S/uL5/uL18 ribosomal complex and an assayed fluorescent-protein landscape. The first two require separately constructed and scored homolog cohorts. The fluorescent-protein assay tests epistasis within one molecule. The machine-readable admission and evaluation rules apply prospectively, regardless of whether sufficient evidence is obtained. [RNA polymerase](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4YG2) · [Ribosome](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/4YBB) · [Poelwijk et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-12130-8)
